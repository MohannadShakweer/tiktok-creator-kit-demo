The 4 Key Signals (Ranked by Importance)

TikTok's algorithm prioritizes 4 signals: 1. COMPLETION RATE (most important) — What % of viewers watch your full video. Aim for 70%+ on videos under 30s, 50%+ on longer videos. 2. ENGAGEMENT — Likes, comments, shares, saves. Comments and shares carry more weight than likes. 3. FOLLOWER INTERACTION — If a viewer's friends engage with your video, it gets pushed to more people. 4. ACCOUNT SIGNALS — Your past performance, consistency, and content quality history. PRO TIP: Optimize for completion rate first. If viewers don't finish your video, nothing else matters.
